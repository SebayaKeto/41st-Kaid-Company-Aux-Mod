Field medicine

New medical items (work with ACE bandaging)
- Kolto Wrap: everyday wrap for scrapes and cuts. Weak on deep wounds, and wounds reopen more often.
- Coagulant Foam: seals deep bleeders (avulsions, punctures, velocity wounds) and rarely reopens. Medics only by default.
- Synthflesh Sheet: for blaster burns and wide surface wounds.
- Field Medicine Crate: 60 Kolto, 30 Synthflesh, 30 Foam, 40 Bacta, 10 tourniquets.

Changes
- Bacta Bandage is now best on surface wounds and burns and weaker on deep wounds. Reopening is unchanged.
- Kit Menu: riflemen carry 12 Kolto, 4 Synthflesh and 6 Bacta instead of 20 Bacta. Medics carry 40 Bacta, 15 Foam, 10 Synthflesh and 10 Kolto instead of 80 Bacta. Kits are lighter.
- CBA settings: who may use Bacta (default anyone) and Coagulant Foam (default medics).
- Bandages never change how much damage it takes to go down.
