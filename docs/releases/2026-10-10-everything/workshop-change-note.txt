Battle droid AI
- B1 squads on assault now march in tight single-rank lines, carrying their rifles in the classic B1 pose, and keep advancing under fire.
- The line fires rolling volleys on the move, with aimed shots in between, instead of a constant trickle.
- Squads cut down to one or two droids join the nearest friendly squad; AT droids keep up with their line and still use their launchers on vehicles.
- Garrisoned and defending droids are unchanged. Squads far from players fight the old way, so large battles cost no more than before.
- New CBA settings under BURNS: drill volleys, line tracks, march speed-up, march pose, pose range and drill range.
