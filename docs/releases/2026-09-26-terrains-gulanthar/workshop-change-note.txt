Terrain and Gulanthar update

- Added "41st Umbara - Ground War Highway" (fst_umbara_highway).
- Added "Umbara - Scar Reach" (fst_umbara_scarreach).
- Added "41st Umbara - Black Lantern" (fst_umbara_lantern).
- Gulanthars: removed the eight-creature population cap. They charge acquired targets from up to 600 m (new CBA setting "Gulanthar charge distance"), close in on the target directly, and a failed psychic attack no longer delays the bite.
- Gulanthars climb hills at a gallop instead of walking (about twice as fast uphill). They no longer freeze for up to 20 seconds when restarting a chase, and they re-engage fleeing targets about three times faster.
- QRF drop pods: B1 squads are immune to damage for their first 5 seconds after landing, so the pods that land right after them no longer kill them.

All other mod files are unchanged. Let Steam finish the update before joining. Update the server and headless clients before loading any of these maps.
