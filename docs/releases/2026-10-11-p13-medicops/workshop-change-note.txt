Medic Ops

New for medics (every part can be switched off under Addon Options > "41st Medic Ops")
- Casualty beacons: downed players call a medic automatically. Anyone can Call Medic / Cancel Medic Call from the ACE self menu.
- Medic HUD: medics see casualty markers with severity, name and distance (500 m), plus map markers and chat alerts.
- Medscanner: scan a casualty (or yourself) for vitals, wounds per body part with the right item to use, and internal bleeding.
- Bacta Mist grenade: a teal cloud that, for 30 s, eases pain and slows bleeding by about 30% for anyone within 6 m. It does not heal.
- Internal bleeding: a heavy torso hit on a player may (25% chance) cause a hidden slow bleed that bacta does not stop. A medic seals it with Coagulant Foam or a Cauterizer.
- Medic report and "Medic of the Op": patients, treatments, revives and more; Zeus can show it with the 41st Medical > Medic Report module.
- Optional bacta charges (off by default): bacta tanks can be set to use charges, refilled with Bacta Cartridges. Medic Resupply Crate added.

Kit Menu: every medic kit gets a Medscanner and 2 Bacta Mist grenades.
